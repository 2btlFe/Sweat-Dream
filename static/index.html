Each episode draws, from its own seed and close to the defaults: the camera's position, angle and field of view, the light, and the room (YAM tabletop or Evening den). The same batch seed gives the same scenes again.

      

      

      
The seed is the whole batch: it gives each episode its own layout (the same episodes again with the same seed; other seeds share none). The scene settings below apply to every episode; the ones marked random are drawn from each episode's seed. Runs in the background.

      
Original: the seed and the environment are fixed: every episode starts from the simulator's default scene (the same object layout) with default scene settings; the seed and the scene settings below are not used. Dream-1 runs with the failure monitor and the recovery script, as in Data collection (its settings above).
